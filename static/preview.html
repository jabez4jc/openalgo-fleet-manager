Design rules

Applies to every screen
Status colour is load-bearingNever use success or danger decoratively. A green pill means a live health check returned 200.
Mono for machine valuesHosts, ports, versions, keys, timestamps and log output are JetBrains Mono. Prose and labels are Inter.
Destructive actions confirmReboot, stop and remove name the exact target and the blast radius in instances.
Density over whitespaceOperators scan 20+ rows at once: 10/14px table cells, 13px rows, no card nested inside a card.
